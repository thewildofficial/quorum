# Demo video script (3-5 minutes)

Follow the guidelines: show the product working, don't read code on camera.

1. **(20s) Problem** - "When I decide something, the reasoning evaporates. Two months later I can't
   check whether my judgment was any good. Deliberate keeps the whole loop: options, weights, scores,
   a stress test, a forecast, and the review."
2. **(40s) Build a decision** - Log in (demo@deliberate.local / demo-password-123), click
   *New decision*, walk the three-step wizard with a real example (e.g. "Which research project
   should I prioritise this semester?"), adding 3 options and 4 weighted criteria.
3. **(40s) Score and rank** - Fill the scoring matrix, save, and show the weighted ranking appear
   with the leader's margin.
4. **(45s) Fragility check** - Point at the sensitivity panel: "Doubling how much I care about
   feasibility nearly ties the race - that tells me the real argument is about feasibility, not the
   other three criteria." Mention the verdict is deliberately scoped to the tested range.
5. **(30s) Commit** - Decide: pick the winner, set 70%, and show that the app *requires* an
   observable success criterion and a review date. Note the scores freeze now.
6. **(30s) Review** - Open a closed decision, show the recorded outcome and its Brier score.
7. **(30s) Calibration** - Open the Calibration tab: mean Brier across decisions, the calibration
   curve, and what the diagonal means.
8. **(15s) Close** - "The point isn't that the app knows the right choice - it doesn't. It makes my
   assumptions explicit enough to be wrong in a way I can learn from."
